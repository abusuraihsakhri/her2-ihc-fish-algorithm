HER2 IHC / dual-probe ISH interpreter

Apply the ASCO/CAP 2018 algorithm, reaffirmed in 2023, to a pathologist-assigned IHC score and optional HER2/CEP17 signal counts. The Python algorithm runs locally inside your browser.

Not a clinical diagnostic system. Independent pathology review, assay quality control and adjudication are required. Do not enter patient identifiers. HER2 status alone does not determine drug eligibility.

Case measurements

Descriptive input; does not calculate the IHC score.

Dual-probe ISH (optional)

Enter both means from at least 20 invasive tumor nuclei, or leave both blank to report IHC only.

The ratio is calculated without rounding before classification.

Independent blinded recount (when indicated)

For IHC 2+ with ISH group 2, 3 or 4, use measurements from a second blinded observer (at least 20 additional nuclei).
